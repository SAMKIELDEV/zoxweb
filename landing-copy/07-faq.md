## Heading
You may be wondering

## Is Zox free?
Yes to start. You get a daily allowance that resets every 24 hours. If you need more, you can buy credits.

## Do I need to install anything?
No. Zox lives in WhatsApp. Open the chat and send a message.

## What can I send?
Text, PDFs, photos, and YouTube links. Zox reads what you send and answers in the chat.

## Can Zox be wrong?
Yes, like any AI. When Zox doesn't know something, it searches the web first. For anything important, double-check.

## What happened to SAMKIEL BOT?
Zox is the next version. If you used SAMKIEL BOT, your account was moved over. Sign in with SAMKIEL ID and set a password the first time.

## Is my data private?
Zox stores your messages so it can answer and keep your history. The privacy policy spells out how we handle your data.
