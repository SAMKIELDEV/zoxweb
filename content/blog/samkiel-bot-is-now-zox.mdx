SAMKIEL BOT is now **Zox**.

Zox still lives on WhatsApp, but it does a different job. SAMKIEL BOT was built for fun in group chats. Zox is a personal AI agent: you message it, and it helps you get things done.

## What Zox does

- **Chats with you** about anything, and remembers the recent conversation so follow-ups make sense.
- **Searches the web** for anything current and comes back with sources.
- **Reads PDFs** and sends back the key points.
- **Reads photos, screenshots and diagrams**, including the text in them.
- **Summarizes YouTube videos** from a link.
- **Listens to voice notes**, and can answer with one.
- **Makes images** when you ask for one.
- **Downloads media** from public social media posts.

## What is gone

Games, group management, stickers, memes and jokes did not come along. Zox works one to one, for you.

## Your account

If you used SAMKIEL BOT, your account has moved to SAMKIEL ID, the sign-in shared by SAMKIEL products. The first time you sign in, you set a new password.
