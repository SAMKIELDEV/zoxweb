# Design audit: landing sections, sign-in, dashboard (November 2026)

Audit only: no code was changed for it. Screenshots are in `docs/design/audit/` (not committed): `landing/` (sections 03 to 09), `signin/`, `dashboard/` (every page, populated), `states/` (loading, error, empty, unlinked), `figma/` (the published design), and `report.json` (the automated checks). Widths 375, 768 and 1280; light and dark.

## What was compared against

- **Figma frames: none.** The three Figma links in the brief were still placeholders, and the Figma connector in this setup cannot open the Samkiel.dev files (it needs signing in to that account).
- **What exists instead:** the Figma Make export of 12 November (`Zox Dashboard Design.zip`, recovered from `zox` history, commit `61ca0c9`), its published copy at `import-social-86577787.figma.site` (captured for sign-in, Home, Billing, Usage and Settings), and `docs/design/landing-reference/` (the *old* landing design).
- **Landing sections 03 to 09 have no frame at all.** They were built from the landing copy brief (`docs/design/landing-copy.zip`), which came after the Figma design and has different sections. Every landing row below is therefore "no frame": those sections need design work before more code.
- The dashboard was captured with the API mocked in the browser (sample account, empty account, unlinked account, a 500 error, and a request that never answers), so every state was reachable without a real session.

## Findings

### Must fix

1. **The Problem heading runs to 4 lines at 375px.** The brief caps headlines at two. Files: `components/marketing/Story.tsx` (Problem), copy in `components/marketing/content.ts` (`PROBLEM.heading`).
2. **Dark mode loses two surfaces.**
   - The footer card: `--color-night` #0C0905 against `--color-paper` #141009 in `app/globals.css`.
   - The bento cards: `card` against `band`.
   Both read as part of the background.
3. **The landing motion budget is mostly unbuilt.** Effects 3 to 6 in DESIGN.md "Marketing motion" (button icon slide, bento illustration hover, FAQ height and opacity, heading reveal) do not exist. Sections 03 to 09 are static. (This was the paused step 5.)
4. **The Pricing teaser has no focal point.** `components/marketing/PricingTeaser.tsx`. It needs a design, not a tweak (see below).
5. **Dashboard direction is undecided.** The Figma export and DESIGN.md disagree on the Home balance card, the stat tiles and the card grouping. Code changes there are guesswork until you pick one (question 2).
6. **Tap targets under 44px on phones** (DESIGN.md section 12):
   - the composer inputs are 41px tall: `components/marketing/Composer.tsx` and `PhoneDemo.tsx`;
   - the user chip is 36px wide: `components/shell/UserChip.tsx`;
   - "Go to Usage" is 79×21px: `components/home/RecentActivity.tsx`.
   "Manage your SAMKIEL ID" and "Go to Billing" are 18px tall, but they are inline text links, which WCAG 2.5.8 allows, so they are polish, not must-fix.

### Should fix

1. **Bento composition.** The drawings are too small for their cards, the large cards are mostly empty, and at 375px it is five identical cards in a row. It reads as generic because it is the stock "feature cards with icons" layout. Files: `Story.tsx`, `Illustrations.tsx`.
2. **One layout repeated.** 04, 06 and 07 share the title-left, text-right split, with no change of scale or density between them.
3. **Trust strip is too quiet** to do its job (the page's only proof).
4. **Button corners disagree.** The landing and sign-in pages use pills (`shape="pill"`), while the dashboard uses `rounded-md` (the default in `components/ui/Button.tsx`), so "Top up" looks like another product. Pick one, or write the rule down in DESIGN.md.
5. **Icon stroke weights.** About 14 different values are in use. `ICON_STROKE` is 1.6 (`components/shell/nav.ts`), but the composer uses 2.0, `BrandIcons` uses 1.8, and the phone uses 1.8 to 2.4. DESIGN.md asks for one stroke weight. The phone is a picture of WhatsApp, so it can be the written exception; the composers cannot.
6. **Dashboard width.** Content is capped at 720px until 1280px, and at 1280px Settings is a 485px column in a 1060px area. That is a lot of empty paper.
7. **Sidebar active state is too faint** compared with the design.
8. **The Usage chart is missing** (needs a backend endpoint for daily totals).
9. **Error states are bare.** They are correct, but they sit as a single line in the corner of an empty page.

### Polish

1. **Off-scale type inside the phone:** 8px on the PDF badge (`ChatBubble.tsx:242`) and 7px on the battery (`PhoneStatusBar.tsx:84`, `:89`). They are inside an illustration, so record the exception in DESIGN.md.
2. **The footer wordmark** is too dominant for decoration.
3. **The CTA glow** is invisible in light mode.
4. **The footer's "Sign in" link** should hide or swap for signed-in visitors, as the header now does.
5. **Font weights:** `font-semibold` appears 13 times in `components/marketing` and on every dashboard heading, and DM Sans bold appears twice in the phone. DESIGN.md section 14 allows three DM Sans weights on the marketing pages. On the dashboard (two weights per family per screen) it looks compliant, but check screen by screen.
6. **Bottom nav on phones:** in full-page captures the fixed bottom nav sits over "Continue to payment" and "Unlink WhatsApp". That is how full-page screenshots draw fixed elements, so it is probably not a real overlap, but check on a phone that the last row clears the bar.
7. **Ignore the "N" badge** over the sidebar's Sign out: it is Next's dev indicator and is not in production.

No raw colours, no default Tailwind palette classes, no hover shadows, no banned words and no exclamation marks were found in components or copy.

## Responsive audit

- **Horizontal scroll:** none, at any width, on any page.
- **Headlines over two lines:** "Your assignment is due at 8. Your notes are in five places." runs to 4 lines at 375px. The 1280px flags on "Free to start." and "Questions" were measuring errors (the grid stretches the heading box); visually they are one line.
- **Tap targets under 44px at 375px:**
  - composer inputs (41px);
  - user chip (36px wide);
  - "Go to Usage" (21px);
  - inline links "Manage your SAMKIEL ID" and "Go to Billing" (18px; allowed as inline text);
  - footer links "Guide", "Blog" and "Sign in" are 44px tall but under 44px wide, which is acceptable for text links in a list.
- **Overlap:** only the fixed bottom nav in full-page captures (see polish 6).

- **03 Trust:** what proof looks like when testimonials and invented numbers are banned. That is a design question, not spacing.
